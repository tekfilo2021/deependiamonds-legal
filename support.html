About Deepen Diamonds India LLP

Built on trust. Driven by value.

Deepen Diamonds India LLP is a manufacturer, wholesaler and distributor of Natural and Lab-Grown Diamonds, serving B2B partners across markets with a commitment to quality, competitive pricing and dependable supply.

Our approach is simple — understand our partners' requirements, help them fulfil their orders efficiently, and enable them to grow their business with confidence. We focus on offering the right diamonds at the right prices, backed by timely supply and responsive service.

Established in 2025, Deepen Diamonds India LLP has already built a growing network of more than 4,000 customers across continents. This journey reflects the trust our partners have placed in us and our commitment to building lasting business relationships.

Our Vision

As the diamond industry continues to evolve, we believe sourcing should evolve with it.

Our app brings the diamond-buying experience to a single, convenient platform, making it easier for our B2B partners to browse, hold and purchase diamonds, anytime and from anywhere. With a user-friendly interface, quick access to our inventory and competitive pricing, the platform is designed to make sourcing faster, simpler and more efficient.

Our vision is to combine the reliability of a trusted diamond partner with the convenience of technology, creating a seamless sourcing experience that saves time, supports profitability and helps our partners serve their customers better.
